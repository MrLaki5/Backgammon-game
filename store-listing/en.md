## Full Description

🎲 **Royal Backgammon** — The ultimate backgammon experience on mobile!

Play against a powerful AI in 4 difficulty levels or challenge a friend on the same device. Beautiful themes, professional ELO rating, and no internet needed.

### Features:

🤖 **Play vs AI (Offline)**
• 4 levels: Easy, Medium, Hard, Royal
• No internet needed — play anywhere, anytime
• True AI with multi-step lookahead analysis

👫 **Local Two-Player (Pass & Play)**
• Share one device between two players
• Board rotates for each player's perspective
• No accounts or internet needed

🎨 **Beautiful Themes**
• 4 visual themes: Royal, Pop Art, Cyberpunk, Persian Luxury
• High-quality graphics and smooth animations

🏆 **ELO Rating System**
• Professional chess-style ranking
• Player statistics and win/loss tracking
• Player profiles with avatars

📱 **More Features:**
• Step-by-step tutorial for beginners
• Save and continue games
• Instant rematch after each game
• Shake to roll dice or use the button
• Persian and English language support
• Adjustable sound and music

🔇 **No Gambling — No Pay-to-Play**
• Pure entertainment
• No coins needed to play
• No daily game limits
